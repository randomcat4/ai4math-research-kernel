\[
(\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y})^{2}\ge 0 .
\]

\[
(\sqrt{x})^{2}-2\sqrt{x}\sqrt{y}+(\sqrt{y})^{2}=x+y-2\sqrt{xy}\ge 0 .

\[
x+y\ge 2\sqrt{xy}.
\]

\[
\frac{x+y}{2}\ge \sqrt{xy}.

Conversely, if \(x=y\) then \(\sqrt{x}=\sqrt{y}\) and \((\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y})^{2}=0\), so the original inequality holds as an equality.
